Indite is dictation into any app, and meetings kept as transcripts, all on your own Mac.

**Dictation.** Hold the Globe key and talk; the words are typed where your cursor is. A ribbon panel shows them as they arrive. Apple's on-device model can clean them up, summarize them or follow a prompt of your own, and read the field you are dictating into so a reply matches its thread. Recent dictations are kept, with an undo key and a type-again key.

**Meetings.** Start Meeting from the menu bar. The other side of a call is recorded through a system audio tap. When the meeting ends, the transcript is split by speaker, titled and summarized. Click a word to hear it, with the word being said set in bold as the audio plays. Correct lines by typing over them, give them to another speaker, or split them. Import recordings; export as Markdown or plain text; deleted meetings wait thirty days in Recently Deleted.

**Rewriting** comes with Simple Clean, which fixes only what the recognizer got wrong and keeps every other word as you said it.

**Nothing leaves your Mac.** Transcription, rewriting and speaker separation run locally. The network is used only to fetch Apple's speech model, the speaker models when you ask for them, and news of a newer Indite.

**Installing it.** Download the disk image below, open it, and drag Indite into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it cannot check Indite for malware, because the app is not signed through Apple's paid developer program. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. After that, Indite updates itself: it downloads each new version and asks you to restart it, and macOS does not ask again. Requires macOS 27.
