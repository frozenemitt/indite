**Help that answers.** Choose Help from Nscribe's menu and ask a question in your own words. Apple's on-device model answers on this Mac from Nscribe's help articles, and a button under the answer opens the setting it names. Browse all articles lists every one by topic, and saying "Search Nscribe for" and a question to Siri opens Help with the answer.

**Your Mac's language.** Nscribe transcribes dictations, meetings and recordings in the first language under Preferred Languages that Apple's speech recognizer supports, such as German, Spanish, French or Japanese. Rewriting keeps your words in that language.

**Frosted glass.** The dictation panel and the meeting pill are Apple's Regular glass, which frosts what is behind them so your words read over anything. Both follow light and dark mode. The Glass and Words and ribbon sliders are gone from Settings.
