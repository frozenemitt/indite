**Recent Dictations has its own window.** Choose Recent Dictations from Indite's menu to see your dictations grouped by day. Each one has Insert, Copy and As Heard buttons, and the top of the window shows the words you have dictated, your speaking speed and your days in a row. The last five are still in the menu, under Type Again.

**Transcribe a dictation again.** Indite keeps the recording of each dictation for 14 days, or as long as you set in Settings, under Dictation. Click Transcribe Again, and Indite hears it again with your current Words and rewriting. A dictation whose recognition failed is kept with its recording, so you can recover the words.

**Indite learns names you correct.** Fix a name Indite typed, such as "Pria" to "Priya", within a minute, and Indite adds it to your Words. A notification says so, with Undo.

**Say "press enter" to send.** End a dictation with "press enter" or "press return", and Indite types the words and presses Return. Say only "press enter" to send what you have already written.

**Snippets.** In Settings, under Words, give a piece of saved text a spoken cue, such as "my calendar link". Say the cue, and Indite types the saved text exactly as written.

**Better sentences.** Indite joins sentences the recognizer cut at a pause, such as "I left early. Because it rained." The AI rewrite may change punctuation and capitals but no longer changes your words, apart from writing a number another way.

**Microphones in order.** Settings lists every microphone Indite has seen, with the Mac's own first, and each dictation records from the first one connected. AirPods no longer take over the moment they connect. If a microphone sends no sound during a dictation, Indite warns you and names it.

**Smaller additions:**

- A mouse side button can start and stop a dictation. Choose it in Settings, under Dictation.
- "Mute the Mac while you dictate" silences music and calls until the dictation ends.
- "Hide the dictation panel from screen sharing", in Settings under Feedback, keeps it out of shared screens and screenshots.
- A short sound and a lock show when a double tap locks the recording.
- Undo is now Control-Command-Z (⌃⌘Z), and Type Again is Control-Command-V (⌃⌘V).

**Fixed:** dictation recorded nothing, with "No sound yet from microphone", when the speakers and the microphone ran at different sample rates.
