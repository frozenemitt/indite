**A dictation survives ⌘Tab.** Once the start sound has played, you can switch apps or press other keys while you hold Option, and letting go types what you said. Before, pressing another key threw the dictation away without a sound. Holding Option to click something, with nothing said, still makes no dictation.

**Settings in plain words.** Every setting now says what it does, and its explanation sits under its name in the same row. "When you stop talking" chooses between typing the words where the cursor is and only copying them. Voice recordings have a section of their own, apart from the list of recent dictations.

**Vocabulary and replacements in one list.** Settings → Words has two lists, Vocabulary and Snippets, each opened with Edit…. Type a word in the field at the top and press Return to add it. Give it a "Replace with", and Indite writes that instead whenever it hears the word. Each entry has its own delete button, and the list can be searched.

**Microphones in order.** The microphone list is numbered and marks the one in use. Drag a microphone to change which one Indite tries first.

**Your own prompts.** In a prompt's settings, the instructions are what you see first. The AI's role and the word-choice settings sit under Advanced, each with a tooltip, and "Copy to Your Prompts" shows only on the built-in prompts.
