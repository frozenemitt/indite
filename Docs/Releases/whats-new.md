**Nscribe is now Indite.** The same app under a new name, from an old word for putting something into words. Nscribe can't update itself into Indite, so [download Indite](https://github.com/frozenemitt/indite/releases/latest), drag it to Applications, and move Nscribe to the Trash. Indite brings over your settings, meetings and recordings the first time it opens. macOS asks again for each permission, because it sees Indite as a new app.

**Help that knows your setup.** Help checks what Indite needs on this Mac: Accessibility, the microphone and speech recognition, the Globe key setting, Apple Intelligence and the speaker models. When something is off, Help's first screen says so, with a button that opens the right settings. Ask why something isn't working, and the answer gives the fix for what Help found.
