**Rewriting works on sensitive subjects.** Apple's model used to refuse some dictations about subjects such as religion, health or anxiety, and Indite pasted them as heard. Indite now asks the model in the way Apple checks less strictly, so those dictations get cleaned up like any other. Meeting summaries and the other prompts gain the same way. In testing, every dictation Apple had refused came back cleaned up.

**A cleanup can't replace your words.** With a prompt that keeps your words, such as Simple Clean, Indite throws out any answer that leaves out most of what you said and pastes your dictation as spoken. This covers the rare case where the model answers with a sentence of its own, such as a refusal, in place of your words.
