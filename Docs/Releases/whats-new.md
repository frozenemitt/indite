**Dictate with the Option key.** Hold the right Option key, talk, and let go. In Settings, under Dictation, you can choose the left Option key or either one instead. Option still works as Option: typing ⌥E, pressing it with another modifier, or holding it while you click or drag throws the recording away silently. The Globe key and recorded key combinations are gone, and so is the macOS setting the Globe key needed.

**Double-tap to keep listening.** In "Hold to talk", tap the key twice quickly and Indite keeps listening after you let go, until your next press. Other keys, ⌘Tab included, no longer end the recording. A tap shorter than 300 ms records nothing and shows nothing: the sound and the panel appear only once you have held the key that long, and the microphone is already listening from the press, so your first word is kept.

**Let an AI agent read your meetings.** An agent such as Claude Code can list and search your meetings and dictations, and read a whole transcript with its summary and speaker names. It can change nothing, and Indite sends nothing on its own. Connect Claude Code with one command, given in Help under "Let an AI agent read your meetings".

These three ideas come from [Hex](https://github.com/anomalyco/hex) by Kit Langton and its contributors. Thank you.
